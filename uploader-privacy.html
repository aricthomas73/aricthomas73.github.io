Privacy Policy for Animation Uploader

Animation Uploader is a personal tool operated by the owner of the Drift Loops YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles and descriptions, add them to playlists, and read the channel's own statistics and comments.

The tool is used only by the channel owner with the owner's own Google account. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information about anyone else. The only data it handles is the video files and text the owner chooses to publish, and the access token Google issues to the owner's account, which is kept on the owner's own computers and used for nothing else.

Use of the YouTube API is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
